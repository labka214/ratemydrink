1. Overview

RateMyDrink ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal drink rating application that lets you log, rate, and track your rum and whiskey experiences. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

By using RateMyDrink, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the app's features.

Data Source Purpose
Google account name & email address Google Sign-In Authentication and identifying your account
Profile information (display name, phone number) You enter it voluntarily Personalising your profile
Drink ratings, notes, dates, prices You enter them Storing your personal drink log
Photos of drinks Your device gallery Attaching images to your drink records
Drink name, category, country, rating Derived from your entries Anonymised leaderboard (see §4)

We do not collect location data, device identifiers, or any data beyond what you explicitly provide.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you securely via Google Sign-In
  • To store and display your personal drink log
  • To calculate and display your achievements (badges)
  • To display aggregated, anonymised drink ratings in the leaderboard
  • To export your data as a CSV file when you request it

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any automated decision-making.

4. Public Leaderboard

The leaderboard shows drink names, categories, countries of origin, and average ratings. It does not display your name, email address, profile photo, personal notes, prices, or any other identifying information.

Each leaderboard entry is linked internally to your user ID (a random string generated by Firebase), which is never visible to other users. If you delete your account or a drink entry, the corresponding leaderboard record is removed.

5. Third-Party Services

RateMyDrink uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In — for authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Authentication — manages your login session securely. Firebase Privacy
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your drink records and profile data on Google's servers.
  • Firebase Storage — stores photos you attach to drink entries.

All data is stored within Google Cloud infrastructure. Google's data processing terms apply to the use of these services.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers with industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to your data is protected by Firebase Security Rules, which ensure that only your authenticated account can read or modify your personal records.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your data, but no internet transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — all your drink records and profile are visible directly in the app
  • Export your data — use the CSV export feature in the app
  • Correct your data — edit any drink entry or your profile at any time
  • Delete your data — delete individual entries within the app; to request full account and data deletion, contact us at the address below

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you may also have rights under GDPR including the right to object to processing and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, your personal data (profile, drink entries, photos) will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days. Anonymised leaderboard entries derived from your ratings are removed at the same time.

9. Children's Privacy

RateMyDrink is intended for adults of legal drinking age in their country of residence. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
